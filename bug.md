  - **Fixed: looping clips pumped.** `CreateTone`/`CreateNoise` applied a fade-in *and* fade-out envelope to every clip, including the looping engine and tyre samples, so a one-second engine loop collapsed to near silence once per second (measured: level at the loop point fell to 0.59 of the clip's RMS, and to 0.047 with a narrow window).
